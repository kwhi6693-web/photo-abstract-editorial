# Optional Series Style Lock

Only load this reference for multi-image series, an editorial set, or an explicit request for consistent visual style. Do not load it for an ordinary single-image task.

## First image

After the first image passes QA, form an internal Style Profile containing:

- abstraction baseline and Creative Freedom baseline;
- typography character and panel language;
- whitespace character and motif density;
- overall editorial tone;
- preferred layout-family tendency.

## Subsequent images

Keep the overall abstraction language, Creative Freedom baseline, typography character, panel language, whitespace character, motif density, and editorial tone. Re-analyze every new source for scene, palette, decisive facts, identity, spatial relationships, motif geometry, and suitable layout.

The lock defines a visual family, not a duplicated template. Do not copy exact motif coordinates, exact palette, exact layout, or exact spatial structure. A new photograph must remain the sole factual source for its own motif and composition.
