# Scene Profiles

Classify the supplied photograph before choosing motif behavior. These are decision aids, not literal drawing recipes. Always preserve the supplied photograph as the sole factual source.

| Profile | Abstraction | Creative Freedom | Identity Preservation | Spatial Fidelity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pure Portrait | 30 | 25 | 90 | 80 |
| Environmental Portrait | 45 | 35 | 75 | 85 |
| Landscape | 65 | 50 | 30 | 90 |

| Street / Crowd | 70 | 55 | 25 | 80 |

| Minimal / Light | 80 | 60 | 20 | 70 |

## Profile behavior

### Pure Portrait

Keep pose direction, head/shoulder relationship, body gravity, silhouette direction, clothing color role, subject/background proportion, light direction, and main negative space. Keep identity cues without facial reconstruction, realistic portrait illustration, literal vector tracing, or invented clothing details. Never reduce the principal subject to an anonymous short vertical mark.

### Environmental Portrait

Balance person identity with environment structure, scale, body direction, horizon/architecture/ground relationship, clothing color role, and lighting relationship. The person and environment must remain traceable as a relationship.

### Landscape

Prioritize horizon, depth, water, mountain, road, large color masses, light/dark hierarchy, dominant axis, atmospheric space, and spatial rhythm. Use abstraction to reduce surface detail, not to invent geography.

### Architecture

Retain one to three identity cues such as a taper, arch, spire, roof/eave, characteristic negative space, or dominant proportion. Omit detailed window grids, facade tracing, and blueprint-like literalism.

### Street / Crowd

Anonymous crowd and background people may become irregular short marks, restrained vertical ink marks, or rhythm clusters. This rule does not apply to a Pure Portrait or Environmental Portrait principal subject.

### Still Life

Keep object scale, planar cues, relative position, dominant contour direction, object/background relationship, and material color role. Do not simulate realistic material or volume.

### Minimal / Light

Prioritize shadow, light direction, small structural cues, negative space, color temperature, and minimal geometry. Keep the result quiet and avoid adding objects.
